# Master Services Agreement — DRAFT (internal, not published)

**Status:** draft for attorney review. Not offered to any customer yet.
**Last updated:** 2026-11-01
**Use:** business customers buying document-preparation or review engagements. Consumer purchases use the public Terms of Service instead.

> This is an internal working draft. The public-facing pages are `legal/terms.html`, `legal/privacy.html`, `legal/refund.html`, and `legal/dpa.html`. This MSA is the paper we sign with a company that wants an order form, invoicing, and negotiated terms.

---

## 1. Parties

This Master Services Agreement ("MSA") is between **Lexora AI LLC**, a United States limited liability company ("Lexora AI", "we"), and the customer named on an Order Form ("Customer", "you"). It takes effect on the date the first Order Form is signed.

## 2. Structure

- This MSA sets the general terms.
- Each **Order Form** states the services, the volume, the price, the term, and any special terms.
- If an Order Form conflicts with this MSA, the Order Form wins for that engagement only.
- The **DPA** (published at `/legal/dpa.html`, or as signed) applies where personal data is processed.

## 3. Services

Lexora AI provides software-assisted document preparation and review. Every deliverable is a **draft for Customer's review**. Lexora AI is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. Customer is responsible for having a qualified reviewer approve every deliverable before it is used, filed, or relied upon.

## 4. Customer responsibilities

- Provide accurate source information and complete the intake.
- Nominate a single point of contact authorised to approve deliverables.
- Review and approve each deliverable within the review window in the Order Form (default 5 business days).
- Do not use the services to give legal advice to third parties or to hold out as authorised to practise law.
- Ensure it has the right to share any document or personal data it uploads.

## 5. Fees and payment

- Fees are stated in the Order Form and are exclusive of taxes.
- Invoices are payable net 30 unless the Order Form says otherwise.
- Late amounts may accrue interest at 1% per month or the maximum lawful rate, whichever is lower.
- Government filing fees are never included; the Customer pays the agency directly.
- Refunds follow the published Refund Policy unless the Order Form states a different rule.

## 6. Term, termination, and suspension

- The MSA runs until all Order Forms expire or either party terminates on 30 days' written notice.
- Either party may terminate immediately for a material breach that is not cured within 15 days of written notice.
- We may suspend access for non-payment after 10 days' written notice, or immediately where continued access would break the law or risk the security of other customers.
- On termination, Customer may export its documents for 30 days; we then delete them per the DPA.

## 7. Confidentiality

Each party will protect the other's confidential information with at least reasonable care, use it only to perform this MSA, and disclose it only to personnel and advisers who need it. Standard carve-outs apply: information that is public, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law (with notice where lawful). These obligations survive for 3 years after termination; trade secrets survive for as long as they remain trade secrets.

## 8. Intellectual property

- **Customer owns** its input documents, its data, and the deliverables produced for it.
- **Lexora AI owns** the software, prompts, models, evaluation methods, and know-how, including anything developed before or independently of the engagement.
- Lexora AI grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the deliverables for its internal business purposes.
- No licence is granted to resell the software or to provide it as a service to third parties.

## 9. Warranties

- **Us:** the services will be performed with reasonable skill and care by suitably qualified personnel, consistent with the published documentation current at the time.
- **You:** you have the right to provide the input data and to grant the licences in this MSA.
- No other warranties, express or implied, including fitness for a particular purpose, except where law does not allow the exclusion.

## 10. Service levels

No SLA is offered at signature. If an Order Form specifies one, it will state the availability target, the measurement method, the exclusions, and the service credits — and it will override this section for that Customer.

## 11. Limitation of liability

- Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential loss, or for lost profits, revenue, or data.
- Each party's aggregate liability under this MSA is capped at the greater of (a) the fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the claim, or (b) US$1,000. For consumer-facing engagements the public Terms cap applies instead.
- The cap does not apply to: fraud or wilful misconduct; breach of confidentiality; infringement indemnity; or anything that cannot lawfully be limited.

## 12. Indemnities

- **We indemnify you** against third-party claims that the software, as supplied by us, infringes their IP — provided you notify us promptly and let us control the defence.
- **You indemnify us** against third-party claims arising from your input data, your breach of §4, or your use of a deliverable outside this MSA.

## 13. Insurance

We will maintain, once purchased, commercial general liability and professional liability (E&O) cover at levels stated in the Order Form, and will provide certificates on request. **Current status: not yet purchased.** We will not sign an Order Form requiring insurance until cover is in force.

## 14. Compliance

Each party will comply with the laws applicable to it, including data-protection law (see the DPA) and, where relevant, anti-bribery and export-control rules. We do not claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, or FedRAMP certification.

## 15. Governing law and disputes

Governed by the laws of the State of Florida, USA, with exclusive jurisdiction in the state and federal courts of Pinellas County. The parties will attempt good-faith resolution for 30 days before filing. **Counsel to confirm the state of formation before this is used.**

## 16. General

Notices in writing to the addresses on the Order Form. Neither party may assign without consent, except in a merger or sale of substantially all assets. This MSA, its Order Forms, the DPA, and the Refund Policy are the entire agreement. Amendments must be signed by both parties. If a clause is unenforceable, the rest survives.
