Technology

Grounded.
Not Magic.

We do not train our own model, and we do not claim one. We build grounded retrieval, prompting, and review around commercial models, called through a single AI Gateway so a venture never hardcodes a provider.

🧠
Model-agnostic by design
Requests go to the Balamonis AI Gateway as a capability tier. Switching providers does not require touching venture code.
🔒
Security controls, honestly stated
Encryption in transit and at rest, row-level security, and audit logging are being implemented. We hold no SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, or FedRAMP certification, and we do not claim one.
🌐
Jurisdiction-aware, not jurisdiction-certified
Documents can be tagged by jurisdiction and the review prompt adjusts. Coverage grows as we validate it; we do not claim complete coverage of any legal system.
⚡
Batch processing with visible progress
Long documents are chunked and processed in batches. Progress and failures are shown rather than hidden behind a spinner.
