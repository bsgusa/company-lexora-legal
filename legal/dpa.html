Data Processing Addendum

Last updated 1 November 2026 · Version 1.0 (draft)

Who is who. You are the controller of the personal data you put into Lexora AI. We are the processor, acting on your documented instructions. If you are an individual using the service for your own paperwork, you are the controller and we are the processor of the same data, and the Privacy Policy does the work of this document.

1. Scope

This addendum applies where Lexora AI LLC processes personal data on your behalf in the course of providing the service. It forms part of the Terms of Service. Where this addendum conflicts with the Terms on a data-protection point, this addendum wins.

2. Our obligations as processor

  • Process personal data only on your documented instructions, including for transfers to a third country, unless law requires otherwise — in which case we tell you first unless the law forbids it.
  • Keep the people who can access the data under confidentiality obligations.
  • Implement the technical and organisational measures in section 5.
  • Help you respond to data-subject requests. If someone contacts us directly, we forward the request to you rather than answering it ourselves, unless you ask us to respond.
  • Help you with security reviews, impact assessments, and prior consultations with a supervisory authority, taking into account the nature of the processing and the information we hold.
  • Tell you without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours of becoming aware, if we discover a personal-data breach affecting your data. The notice will describe what happened, what data was affected, what we have done, and what we recommend you do.
  • At your choice, delete or return all personal data at the end of the service, and delete existing copies unless law requires retention.
  • Make available the information you need to demonstrate compliance, and allow an audit as set out in section 6.

3. Your obligations as controller

  • Make sure you have a lawful basis to give us the data, including consent from any third person whose information appears in a document you upload.
  • Give us accurate instructions. We are not obliged to follow an instruction that would break the law.
  • Do not send special-category data unless we have agreed it in writing and the security measures support it.

4. Sub-processors

You give general written authorisation for the sub-processors listed at Sub-processors. We will give you at least 30 days' notice before adding or replacing a sub-processor that processes your documents, so you can object. If you object on reasonable data-protection grounds and we cannot accommodate you, you may terminate the affected service and we refund the unused portion.

We impose data-protection obligations on each sub-processor that are no less protective than this addendum, and we remain liable to you for their performance.

5. Security measures

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest for stored documents and database records.
  • Row-level security so one customer's records cannot be read by another.
  • Least-privilege access; production secrets held in environment configuration, never in source code.
  • Authentication by single-use magic link; no passwords stored.
  • Application and access logging, with logs retained for 30 days.
  • Dependency and secret scanning in the build pipeline.
  • A documented incident-response path, including the 72-hour notice in section 2.

What we do not have: SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, or FedRAMP certification, and no independent penetration-test report we can share yet. We will not describe the service as “certified” or “HIPAA compliant”. If your engagement requires one of those, tell us before signing — the honest answer today is no.

6. Audits

We will answer a reasonable security questionnaire once a year at no charge, and we will provide the evidence we hold (architecture notes, access-control description, scan results). An on-site audit needs 30 days' notice, a signed confidentiality agreement, and a scope agreed in advance; we may charge for time beyond one day per year.

7. International transfers

Processing happens in the United States. Where the data originates in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, the parties will rely on the applicable standard contractual clauses, incorporated by reference, with the optional docking clause applied where relevant. Ask us for the current transfer documentation before you upload anything sensitive.

8. Liability

Each party's liability under this addendum is subject to the limitations in the Terms of Service, except where data-protection law says otherwise.

9. Term

This addendum stays in force for as long as we process personal data for you, and the deletion obligation in section 2 survives termination.
