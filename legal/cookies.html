Cookie & Storage Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

No advertising or analytics cookies. This site does not run Google Analytics, advertising pixels, session recording, heatmaps, or A/B testing tools. That is why you have not seen a consent banner — there is nothing optional to consent to. If we ever add an optional tracker, we will ask first.

1. What is stored in your browser

NameTypePurposeLifetime
sb-<project-ref>-auth-token localStorage Keeps you signed in after the magic-link email. Contains your session token, not your password. Until you sign out, or the session expires

There are no first-party cookies set by this website. If you never sign in, nothing is stored.

2. Third-party requests

These are not cookies, but loading the page makes a request to each of them, which means they see your IP address and user agent:

ServiceWhat it serves
Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com)The Inter typeface
jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net)The Supabase JavaScript client used for sign-in
unpkg (unpkg.com)The AOS scroll-animation stylesheet
WhatsApp (wa.me)Only if you click a “Chat on WhatsApp” link — then Meta's terms apply

None of these sets an advertising identifier for us, and we do not receive a profile from them.

3. How to remove what is stored

  • Sign out using the link in the dashboard, which clears the session token.
  • Clear site data in your browser settings (Chrome: Settings → Privacy → Third-party cookies → See all site data; Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data).
  • Block third-party requests with a content blocker. The site will still work, but fonts may fall back to a system typeface.

4. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We do not track you across sites, so there is nothing for DNT or GPC to switch off. We honour both signals in the sense that no cross-site tracking happens either way.
