Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026 · Effective immediately for this website

The short version. We collect what you type into our forms, the account email you sign in with, and any document you choose to upload. We use it to prepare and review your paperwork. We do not sell it, we do not run advertising trackers, and you can ask us to delete it at any time by emailing support@lexoraai.org.

1. Who we are

Lexora AI LLC (“Lexora AI”, “we”, “us”) is a United States limited liability company. We publish this website and operate the document-preparation software behind it. We are not a law firm and we do not provide legal advice — see our Terms of Service.

Privacy contact: support@lexoraai.org · (727) 260-1783.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact and demo requests: first and last name, work email, phone (optional), organization, plan and service of interest, preferred contact method, and your message.
  • Immigration intake: name, email, phone, form type, current immigration status, priority date (if any), country of birth, country of citizenship, a description of your situation, and which documents you say you already have.
  • Account: the email address you use for a magic-link sign-in. We do not store passwords.
  • Case files: only the documents you choose to upload after signing in.
What we deliberately do not ask for on the public forms: Social Security numbers, A-numbers, passport numbers, or payment card details. Do not put those in a free-text box. If a form later requires an identifier, we will ask for it inside the authenticated case workspace, not on a public page.

Information collected automatically

  • Standard server and application logs from our hosting and database providers: IP address, browser and device type, pages requested, timestamps, and error traces.
  • If you sign in, a session token stored in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in.

We do not run Google Analytics, advertising pixels, session recording, or cross-site trackers. See our Cookie & Storage Policy for the exact list.

4. Who processes it

We use a small set of service providers. The current list, with locations and purposes, is published at Sub-processors. In summary:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage for the site and case workspace.
  • Vercel — website hosting and delivery.
  • Commercial AI model providers, reached through our own AI gateway — used to draft and check documents. Where a provider's terms allow it, we configure the provider not to train on submitted content.
  • Content delivery networks (Google Fonts, jsDelivr, unpkg) — serve fonts and JavaScript libraries. They see your IP address as part of any web request.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We disclose data to authorities only where legally required, and we will tell you unless we are prohibited from doing so.

6. Your choices and rights

Email support@lexoraai.org to:

  • access a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct something that is wrong;
  • delete your intake, case files, or account;
  • export your documents in a portable format;
  • object to a particular use, or withdraw consent you previously gave.

We respond within 30 days. We may need to verify that the request comes from you. We will not charge for a first request. If you are in the EU or UK you may also complain to your local supervisory authority; in California you may exercise the rights in the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not discriminate against anyone for doing so.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is restricted by row-level security and least-privilege credentials, and secrets live in environment configuration rather than source code. We hold no SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, or FedRAMP certification and we do not claim one. Our current posture is described in Security Status. If you find a vulnerability, email security@lexora.ai.

8. Where data is processed

Our providers process data in the United States. If you are outside the US and we need to transfer your data, we rely on the provider's standard contractual clauses or an equivalent lawful mechanism. Ask us for the current transfer documentation before you upload anything sensitive.

9. Children

The service is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has submitted information, email us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

When this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, for account holders, email the address on file at least 14 days before the change takes effect. Previous versions are available on request.
