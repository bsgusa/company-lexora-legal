Security Status

Last updated 1 November 2026 · Reviewed each session that touches authentication, storage, or deployment

We hold no certifications. SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and FedRAMP are not held. We are not “in the process of becoming compliant” in a way that implies an audit is underway — a readiness programme is tracked internally with evidence, and that is all. If a vendor questionnaire asks, the answer is “not certified”.

Implemented

ControlHow it works
Encryption in transitTLS on every page and API call; HSTS-style headers set at the host.
Encryption at restProvided by the database and storage layer for stored records and files.
Row-level securityPostgres RLS policies on user tables; a customer cannot read another customer's rows with the public key.
Least-privilege keysThe browser receives only the publishable key. Service-role keys stay server-side.
Passwordless authSingle-use magic links. No password database to leak.
Secret hygieneSecrets live in environment configuration, never in source. A CI job fails the build if a key-shaped string appears in the repo.
Security headersX-Content-Type-Options, X-Frame-Options: DENY, Referrer-Policy, and a restrictive content policy on static assets.
Dependency scanningAutomated dependency alerts and a lockfile committed for every deploy.
Access loggingApplication and database logs retained 30 days.

What we will not say

  • “Bank-grade”, “military-grade”, or “enterprise-grade” — meaningless without a specification.
  • “HIPAA compliant” — we do not sign BAAs and do not handle protected health information.
  • “GDPR certified” — there is no such certification.
  • “Zero-knowledge” — we process documents to draft from them, so we can read them. Saying otherwise would be false.
  • “Your data is never used for training” — unless the provider contract says so, we say “we configure providers not to train on submitted content where their terms allow it”.

Reporting a vulnerability

Email security@lexora.ai with steps to reproduce. We acknowledge within 2 business days and will tell you what we fixed. We will not pursue legal action against someone who reports a vulnerability in good faith, stays within their own account, and does not access other people's data. We do not currently run a paid bounty.

Incident response

If we confirm a breach affecting customer data, we notify affected customers without undue delay and within 72 hours of becoming aware, describing what happened, what data was involved, what we have done, and what we recommend. See the DPA for the contractual version.
