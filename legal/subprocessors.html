AI providers, specifically

We route model requests through our own gateway so that a venture does not hardcode a provider. Where a provider's terms allow it, we configure the account so submitted content is not used to train models. We will name the specific providers in a signed DPA before we process client documents at volume, and we will not claim a provider “never retains anything” unless their contract says so.

How to object

Email support@lexoraai.org with the vendor name and your data-protection reason. We respond within 15 days. If we cannot resolve it, you may terminate the affected service and we refund the unused portion.
