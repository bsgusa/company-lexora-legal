Refund Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026 · Applies to every paid product and pilot

Today, nothing is refundable because nothing is chargeable. Lexora AI is in early access and does not collect payment through this site. When billing opens, the rules below apply. They are written to be the same rules that appear on the pricing page.

1. Free pilots

A pilot is free. No payment is taken, so there is nothing to refund. If you decide not to continue, tell us and we stop; you keep the evaluation report we produced for you.

2. Monthly and annual subscriptions

When you cancelWhat you get
Within 14 days of your first paymentFull refund of that payment, no questions asked.
After 14 days, before the next renewalNo refund of the current period. Access continues to the end of the period you already paid for.
After a renewal chargeFull refund of the renewal if you tell us within 7 days of the charge and have not used the service in that period.
Annual plans cancelled mid-termRefund of unused whole months, minus any discount you received for paying annually.

We do not pro-rate a monthly plan part-way through a month. If we remove a feature you are paying for, we will refund the unused portion of that period whether or not you ask.

3. Per-document engagements

StageWhat you get
Before we start workFull refund.
Work started, package not delivered50% refund.
Package delivered and acceptedNo refund, except as set out in the next row.
We made a material error in our workWe fix it at no cost, or refund it in full — your choice.

4. What is never refundable by us

  • Government filing fees. USCIS and other agencies set and collect these. They are paid by you, directly to the agency. We cannot refund money we never received, and the agency's own refund rules apply.
  • Fees paid to a third party — for example a translation service, a notary, or a licensed attorney you engaged separately. Those are between you and them.
  • Charges already refunded — obviously.

5. How to ask

  1. Email support@lexoraai.org with the subject line “Refund” and the email address or invoice number on the payment.
  2. We acknowledge within 2 business days and tell you the decision, in writing, within 5 business days.
  3. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 10 business days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

You do not have to give a reason. If you do, it helps us fix the thing that went wrong.

6. Chargebacks

Please email us before you call your bank. A chargeback takes longer for you and costs us a fee, and we can almost always resolve it faster directly.

7. Your statutory rights

This policy sits on top of your legal rights, not instead of them. If consumer law where you live gives you a stronger right to cancel or a longer cooling-off period, that law wins. Nothing here asks you to waive a right you cannot waive.

8. Changes

We will not change this policy retrospectively for a payment already made. The version in force on the date of your payment is the version that applies to it.
